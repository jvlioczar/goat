Transparency

Privacy policy

GOATs is a reference site: you browse the lists without creating an account, filling in forms or seeing ads.

What the site stores

  • No personal data is collected, sold or shared by us. There is no sign-up, no login and no forms.
  • Theme preference: if you pick dark or light mode, that choice is saved only in your browser (localStorage, key goats-theme). It is never sent anywhere, and you can remove it by clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Filters and searches live in the page address itself (for example, ?fam=combate), so you can share a link with the same view. We do not log any of it.
  • No tracking cookies, no analytics tools and no advertising.

External services

To work, the pages load files from a few services. As with any visit on the internet, they receive technical connection data such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site and may keep technical access logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the Gabarito and Figtree typefaces. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • jsDelivr serves the country flags (flag-icons library). See the jsDelivr privacy policy.
  • BiblioVerse (biblioverse.org) serves the navigation bar at the top, which stores no data and uses no cookies.

The sources pages and the footer link to third-party sites (newspapers, federations, Wikipedia, LinkedIn). When you open one, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) you can ask how personal data is handled. Since GOATs stores no personal data, we hold no records about you to look up, correct or delete. Questions can be sent to the project’s maintainer, Julio Cesar Prava.

Changes

If the site ever starts using a service that handles data (for example, a visitor statistics tool), this page will be updated first. The date of the last review is shown in the footer.
